# Artisans Labs

**Software engineering and applied AI, built in Kigali for Africa.**

Artisans Labs is the trading name of **Kigali Web Artisans Ltd**, a Rwandan technology company. We build practical software that solves everyday problems for people and organisations in Rwanda and East Africa, in the languages they speak.

---

## Our main product: Mbaza Ubutabera AI

**Mbaza Ubutabera** ("Ask about justice") is an AI legal assistant that helps people understand Rwandan law in **Kinyarwanda**, English and French.

- **Ask in plain language** on WhatsApp or the web.
- **Every answer points to the law it is based on**, so users can check the source.
- **For individuals, law firms, legal clinics and universities.**

**Try it:** WhatsApp **+250 799 548 557**

> Mbaza provides legal information for research and learning. It does not replace advice from a qualified lawyer.

## How we work

- **Local first.** We design for Rwandan users: Kinyarwanda, mobile money, WhatsApp and affordable phones.
- **Ship small, learn fast.** Small releases, real user feedback, steady improvement.
- **Cite the source.** In legal tech, trust comes from showing where an answer comes from.
- **Grow people.** We hire early-career engineers and help them grow into full-stack builders. We are an employer in the **Mastercard Foundation Associates Program**.
